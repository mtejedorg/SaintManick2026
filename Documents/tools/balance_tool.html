Threat model — MODELLED, not measured

Per enemy group: threat = min(cap,n)/n · Σ(dmg ÷ cycle s) · Σ(effHP ÷ DPS), then × exposure factor. Cycle = beats × 60 / BPM. Exposure 0.13 is calibrated so the Enforcer/Headliner at shipped HP land near the old tool's mini-boss/boss rows (55 / 110). DPS 30 (≈ named-route DPS ~50, test_batch_k K1, × ~60 % uptime — playtest), Enforcer/Headliner cycles and the exposure factor are placeholders for the playtest gauntlet (HANDOFF H6). Combo DPS gains are unmeasured (D3) and default to 0. Hazards (pistons, bomb grid, kill wall, doors) are not in the threat.
