# audio/ambience/

Drop .ogg ambience loops here. AudioManager loads them by name via:
  res://audio/ambience/<clip_name>.ogg

Expected files (from audio brief):
  industrial_hum.ogg     — Zones 1-7: low industrial hum, distant machinery, electrical buzz
  rooftop_wind.ogg       — Zones 8-10: wind sweep, distant city noise, high-altitude atmosphere
