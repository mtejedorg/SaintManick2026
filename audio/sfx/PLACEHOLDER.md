# audio/sfx/

Drop .ogg SFX files here. AudioManager loads them by name via:
  res://audio/sfx/<clip_name>.ogg

Expected files (from audio brief):
  hit_light.ogg          — Nick light attack lands on enemy
  hit_heavy.ogg          — Nick heavy bionic arm lands (distorted thud + electric crackle)
  hit_react.ogg          — Enemy flinch/stagger on hit
  death_enemy.ogg        — Enemy death (body drop + neon fizzle)
  death_nick.ogg         — Nick death sting (low drone, cut to silence)
  combo_tick.ogg         — Combo counter increment blip
  boss_bar_appear.ogg    — Boss health bar slides in
  pause_open.ogg         — Pause menu opens
  pause_close.ogg        — Pause menu closes
  footstep_metal.ogg     — Nick footstep on metal (zones 1-7)
  footstep_concrete.ogg  — Nick footstep on concrete/rooftop (zones 8-10)
  dash.ogg               — Nick dash/dodge whoosh
